# Candidate Bangladesh plastic-waste data sources

Review and cite primary material from the Department of Environment, Bangladesh, Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, city corporations and published technical studies. For waste composition and collection, identify geography, sampling method, year, polymer category and whether estimates represent generated, collected, sorted or residual waste.

For pricing, obtain dated supplier/offtaker quotations and distinguish post-consumer feedstock from sorted flakes, pellets or fuel products. For plant economics, obtain vendor quotations and energy/water/transport rates. A literature yield is not a commercial guarantee.

The base-case configuration is illustrative and must not be treated as verified Bangladesh market data or an investment recommendation.
