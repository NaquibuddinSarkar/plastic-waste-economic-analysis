# Bangladesh plastic-waste context and data sources

The following sources provide policy and sector context. They are not project-specific feedstock contracts, process yields, product prices or equipment quotations.

| Source | Potential use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [Department of Environment (DoE), Multi-sectoral Action Plan for Sustainable Plastic Management in Bangladesh](https://doe.gov.bd/pages/publications/multi-sectoral-action-plan-for-sustainable-plastic-management-in-bangladesh-c9f642-6922da8a81fc96cef9eb684e) | National policy context, baseline and management priorities | Record report year and original definitions; do not treat historic estimates as current market data |
| [World Bank: Meeting Bangladesh's Plastic Challenge through a Multisectoral Approach](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2021/12/23/meeting-bangladesh-s-plastic-challenge-through-a-multisectoral-approach) | Background on plastic waste management and policy pathways | Context material, not facility-specific economics |
| [World Bank: Extended Producer Responsibility for Advancing Circular Economies for Plastics in Bangladesh](https://documents.worldbank.org/curated/en/099121624201515863/pdf/P175908-d4a8d387-3aa2-45e8-899a-c0b8bfb342ad.pdf) | EPR and circular-economy policy analysis | Check exact scope and publication date |
| [MoEFCC policy and rules portal](https://moef.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6940329b35ce18e1c055f3d8) | Check the current legal and regulatory framework | Obtain current legal advice and authority confirmation before project implementation |

## Project-specific data that must be obtained separately

- representative feedstock audit by polymer type, contamination and moisture;
- supplier volume, quality and collection-radius evidence;
- process design and independently validated mass balance;
- product quality specifications and buyer/offtake quotations;
- equipment, civil works, utilities, installation and commissioning quotations;
- transport, labour, insurance, maintenance, residue and utility costs;
- permits, emissions, wastewater, occupational health and process-safety requirements.

Do not populate the model with sector-wide figures when the financial input is plant-specific. Record the source, date, unit, location, quality grade and contractual status for every material input.
